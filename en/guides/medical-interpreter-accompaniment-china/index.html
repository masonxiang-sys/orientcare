Need

Not every visit requires the same level of support

A simple review may need only pre-visit guidance and limited help. A complex case, first visit to a large hospital, or family traveling with a child may need more structured accompaniment.

The decision depends on language, specialty, patient condition, document volume, hospital campus, and familiarity with China.

Coverage

What accompaniment can cover

Support may include reception, registration, department navigation, explanation of administrative steps, payment assistance, general communication with desks, and organization of later instructions.

During a consultation, language support should aim for clarity, not change the doctor's message. Clinical doubts should be directed to the healthcare professional.

Boundaries

What an accompanier should not do

An accompanier should not diagnose, recommend treatment, change medical instructions, interpret results as a professional, or promise outcomes. They should not hide information from the patient or physician.

If the case requires specialized clinical interpretation, more advance planning may be needed, and the accepted format should be confirmed with the hospital.

Preparation

How to use language support well

Before the visit, prepare a short summary, medication list, allergies, and main questions. During the visit, speak in clear sentences and confirm key points: tests, payments, timing, results, and follow-up.

Afterward, review notes and received documents to see what remains pending. OrientCare can help organize this communication without replacing the doctor.

Checklist

Service boundary

What this guide is and is not

This guide helps with preparation, language, documents, and logistics. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with licensed healthcare professionals.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a medical accompanier the same as a doctor?

No. Accompaniment supports language, logistics, and general communication. Medical care belongs to the hospital and its professionals.

Can support include registration and payments?

Yes, when coordinated and allowed by the hospital, support may include administrative steps such as registration, navigation, and payments.

Which languages does OrientCare support?

OrientCare focuses on Spanish and English support for international patients in China.
