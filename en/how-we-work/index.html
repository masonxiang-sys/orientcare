How we work

A clearer process before, during, and after the hospital visit.

OrientCare turns a complex medical trip into a practical sequence: understand the need, prepare documents, coordinate the appointment, accompany the patient when possible, and organize follow-up communication.

Start coordination Medical disclaimer

Step 1

We understand the request

The first message should include the destination city, preferred language, medical specialty or main concern, approximate dates, and the documents already available. This lets us identify what must be clarified before contacting a hospital or preparing a visit.

Step 2

We organize documents and requirements

  • Previous reports, imaging, laboratory results, medication lists, allergies, and prior procedures are organized into a practical sequence.
  • When useful, we suggest an English summary or translation so hospital teams can review the information more efficiently.
  • We separate confirmed information from questions that should be answered by a physician during the consultation.

Step 3

We coordinate the visit route

Depending on the hospital and specialty, coordination may include appointment channels, registration requirements, payment steps, route planning, and language support. Availability and hospital rules vary, so each case needs confirmation before the patient travels.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How early should I contact OrientCare?

As early as possible, especially for complex specialties or travel from abroad. More time makes document preparation and appointment coordination easier.

Can OrientCare accompany me inside the hospital?

We can coordinate on-site support depending on schedule, hospital rules, and the patient's needs. This support is logistical and language-focused.

Does OrientCare handle follow-up after the visit?

We can help organize next steps, reports, and general communication, but treatment decisions remain with the patient and medical professionals.
