Preparation

Before requesting an appointment

  1. 1

    Prepare previous diagnoses, echocardiogram, ECG, stress test, CT, MRI, or catheterization reports if available.

  2. 2

    List current medications, allergies, and prior surgeries with doses and approximate dates.

  3. 3

    Clarify the main goal: second opinion, check-up, surgery, report review, or follow-up.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does OrientCare choose the hospital for me?

We help compare options based on your need, language, documents, and availability, but medical decisions and the final choice remain with the patient and healthcare professionals.

Can I travel with Spanish-language reports?

You can share them to organize the initial information. In some cases, an English summary or translation is helpful before the consultation.

Does accompaniment include the consultation?

We can coordinate on-site support for registration, hospital navigation, and general communication during the visit, depending on schedule and hospital rules.
