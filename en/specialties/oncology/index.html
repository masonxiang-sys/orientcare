Key information for oncology visits

  • The consultation often depends on pathology, clinical stage, imaging, and previous treatments, not only the diagnosis name.
  • Organize surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, immunotherapy, or other treatments in chronological order.
  • If you need a second opinion, explain which decision you need to make after the consultation.

Before the visit

What to expect from the process

The process usually starts with a review of available information: reason for consultation, destination city, approximate dates, previous reports, and language preferences. With that foundation, OrientCare can help organize next steps and explain what should be confirmed before requesting an appointment.

In large Beijing hospitals, a medical visit may include registration, document checks, payment steps, movement between buildings, consultation, additional tests, and follow-up. Our role is to make that route clearer for international patients while keeping administrative coordination separate from clinical decision-making.
