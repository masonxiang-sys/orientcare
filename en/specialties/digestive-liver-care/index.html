Before the visit

What to expect from the process

The process usually starts with a review of available information: reason for consultation, destination city, approximate dates, previous reports, and language preferences. With that foundation, OrientCare can help organize next steps and explain what should be confirmed before requesting an appointment.

In large Beijing hospitals, a medical visit may include registration, document checks, payment steps, movement between buildings, consultation, additional tests, and follow-up. Our role is to make that route clearer for international patients while keeping administrative coordination separate from clinical decision-making.

Patients should also note whether symptoms relate to meals, medication, travel, previous infections, or earlier procedures, because these details make the appointment request more complete.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you coordinate endoscopy or colonoscopy?

We can help prepare the request and understand general requirements, but indication and scheduling depend on the hospital and doctor.

What if my reports are in Spanish?

You can share them to organize the case. In some scenarios, an English summary or translation is useful.

Does OrientCare decide whether I need a procedure?

No. That decision belongs exclusively to the specialist and hospital.
