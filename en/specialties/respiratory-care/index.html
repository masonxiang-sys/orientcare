FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with pediatric respiratory patients?

Yes, we can guide document preparation and coordination for international families.

Should I bring original imaging?

When possible, bring digital files and written reports. Each hospital may have different requirements.

Does OrientCare handle urgent breathing difficulty?

We are not an emergency service. For acute breathing difficulty, contact emergency medical services or go to a hospital.
