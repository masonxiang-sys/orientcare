Practical criteria

What to review before traveling with a child

  • Families should prepare age, weight, vaccines, allergies, medications, and family history in an easy-to-read format.
  • If the case requires a subspecialty, explain the main reason for the visit and the reports that support it.
  • When parents or guardians travel, clarify who will accompany the child and which identity or authorization documents may be needed.

OrientCare

How OrientCare supports families

  • We help turn scattered history into a clear list for the pediatric consultation.

Before the visit

What to expect from the process

The process usually starts with a review of available information: reason for consultation, destination city, approximate dates, previous reports, and language preferences. With that foundation, OrientCare can help organize next steps and explain what should be confirmed before requesting an appointment.

In large Beijing hospitals, a medical visit may include registration, document checks, payment steps, movement between buildings, consultation, additional tests, and follow-up. Our role is to make that route clearer for international patients while keeping administrative coordination separate from clinical decision-making.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with pediatric subspecialties?

We can guide the search and preparation based on the need described, using available information and hospital coordination when possible.

Which documents should we bring?

Medical history, vaccines, recent reports, imaging, lab tests, and medication lists. If documents are in Spanish, an English summary may help.

Does OrientCare handle emergencies?

We are not an emergency service. For emergencies, contact local medical services or the relevant hospital directly.
