Privacy

How OrientCare handles patient information.

Patients often need to share sensitive information before a hospital visit. This policy explains what OrientCare may collect, why we use it, how it may be shared for coordination, and how patients can request updates or deletion.

Ask a privacy question Medical disclaimer

Information

What we may receive

  • Contact details such as name, email, phone or messaging account, country, preferred language, and travel timing.
  • Medical coordination details such as specialty, stated diagnosis or symptoms, prior reports, imaging, prescriptions, allergies, and questions for the hospital.
  • Administrative details such as destination city, appointment preferences, hospital responses, payment flow questions, and accompaniment needs.

Use

Why we use this information

OrientCare uses patient information to respond to inquiries, prepare document checklists, coordinate appointment logistics, support communication with hospitals when authorized, plan accompaniment, and organize follow-up steps.

Sharing

When information may be shared

Medical documents or personal details may be shared with hospitals, translators, or coordination partners only when needed for the requested service and with patient or family authorization. We do not sell patient information.

Patient control

How to protect your information

  1. 1Send carefully

    Share only documents needed for coordination and remove unrelated information when possible.

  2. 2Confirm consent

    Tell us before documents may be sent to a hospital or coordination partner.

  3. 3Request changes

    Email hello@orientcare.org to update, correct, or request deletion of inquiry information.

Freshness

Review and update note

This page is reviewed as OrientCare updates its coordination process, service boundaries, or privacy practices.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to send medical documents immediately?

No. You can first describe the situation generally. Documents are usually needed only when preparing a specific hospital route or appointment request.

Does OrientCare sell patient information?

No. Patient information is used for coordination and communication related to the requested service.

Can I ask OrientCare to delete my information?

Yes. Email hello@orientcare.org with the request. Some information may need to be retained where required for service records or legal compliance.

Contact

Questions about privacy or documents?

Contact us before sending sensitive files if you want to confirm what is needed for coordination.
